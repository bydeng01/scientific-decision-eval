Randomized Crossover Experimental Design
Similarity in storm conditions affecting both ranges allow for the possibility of a “cross-over” design resulting in paired data (seeded and control cases), and statistically is the most efficient design to conduct and evaluate.

value is greater than 0.05, the statistical test 
does not reject the null hypothesis that there is no effect of 
cloud seeding.
